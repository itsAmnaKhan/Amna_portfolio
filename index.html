About me

Empowering healthcare through engineering

Greetings from Germany! This Pakistani girl from Rawalpindi first landed in Türkiye with a Türkiye Bursları Scholarship and a lot of plans, and is now based in Augsburg.

My time in Türkiye meant learning Turkish, making friends from many countries and, yes, discovering that I'm obsessed with trying every cuisine I can find. From Turkish delights to Pakistani karahi, good food has become my universal language for connecting with people.

As a biomedical engineer, my goal is to lead projects that improve people's lives. I bring diverse teams together around a shared vision, whether we're testing a new approach to a healthcare problem or I'm mentoring others to take on harder problems with me. Working across cultures has taught me that the best ideas come when different perspectives meet around one goal, and I bring that same collaborative leadership to every project, linking disciplines, cultures and complex problems.

Medical Imaging Intern (Erasmus+)

Dice 0.78 → 0.84−62% false positivesPSMA-PET/CT
  • Designed an anatomy-guided post-processing pipeline for prostate cancer PSMA-PET/CT segmentations, cutting false-positive lesions by 62% without losing true lesions.
  • Built Python evaluation workflows with CADS organ priors in the MUSIQ framework on a Slurm HPC cluster; benchmarked three models against physician-revised masks across two cohorts.
  • Reviewed failure cases with radiologists in ImFusion. Paper submitted to BVM 2027.
